You don't need to read Tamil to teach it

Every Tamil word on this site has the Tanglish (Tamil written in English letters) right under it, and the English meaning below that. Tap any card to hear it spoken. Read the Tanglish, listen, and say it together with your child.

1

Learn the sound with them

Open a lesson, tap the speaker, and repeat it together. You only need the Tanglish line. Your child reads the Tamil line.

2

Hide the helpers to test

Untick Tanglish and English at the top. Now your child must read the Tamil alone. You can still tick them back to check.

3

Use it for school homework

For any page of the school book, open Homework helper. Copy the Tamil text with Google Lens and paste it in to get Tanglish and the meaning.

4

Practise for 15 minutes a day

Short and daily works better than long and weekly. Use the Practice quiz and letter tracing at the end of each session.

How to read the Tanglish

A double letter means a longer sound: a is short (like "cup"), aa is long (like "father"). ee is "see", oo is "moon". A line on top (ē, ō) also means long: ē like "cake", ō like "boat". zh is the special Tamil sound in "Tamizh": curl the tongue back and say a soft "r".

Tamil has three "n" letters, two "l" letters and two "r" letters. In the letter charts the hard ones are written with a capital letter: N (ண), L (ள), R (ற). Inside words they are written in small letters, the way people type Tamil on WhatsApp.

Letters எழுத்துகள்

For Class 2: learn the whole uyirmei chart, one row a day. Then read the words with vowel signs in the Words tab.
For Class 1: learn the 12 vowels first, then the 18 consonants. In the big chart, only the first two columns (க, கா…) are needed this year.

Tamil has 12 vowels (உயிர் எழுத்துகள்), 18 consonants (மெய் எழுத்துகள்) and one special letter ஃ. A consonant joined with a vowel makes an uyirmei letter (உயிர்மெய்): க் + அ = க.

Vowels

உயிர் எழுத்துகள்uyir ezhutthugal · 12 letters

Consonants

மெய் எழுத்துகள்mei ezhutthugal · 18 letters

The dot on top (புள்ளி, pulli) means the letter has no vowel sound. Say it very short.

ஃ (aaytham, ahk) is a rare special letter that sounds like a soft "h", as in அஃது (ahdhu).

Uyirmei chart

உயிர்மெய் எழுத்துகள்18 × 12 = 216 letters

Each row is one consonant joined with all 12 vowels. Tap a letter to hear it. Learn one row a day: ka, kaa, ki, kee, ku, koo…
